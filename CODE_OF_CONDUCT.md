# Code of conduct

This project exists to make public housing data usable by the people it affects
most, many of whom are dealing with unsafe housing while they use it. Behave
accordingly.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Both are possible at once.
- Assume the person you are replying to knows something you do not.
- Critique the code, the data, or the decision — not the person.
- Remember that issues here often involve someone's actual home.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Discrimination or slurs relating to race, ethnicity, national origin,
  immigration status, religion, disability, age, gender, gender identity, or
  sexual orientation.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including tying a specific address
  lookup to a named individual.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to **hello@howellandgibbs.com**. Reports are
read by the maintainers and kept confidential. You do not need to be sure it
was a violation to report it.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow this document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe behavior.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits —
and anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Attribution
